# 🧛‍♂️ WnV_the_game 🐺

**WnV_the_game** is a team project developed for an Object-Oriented Programming course.  
The assignment was to design and implement a simple **RPG-style game** based on the classic clash between **Vampires and Werewolves**.

---

## 🎮 Game Concept

Players choose to join either:

- **Vampire Clan 🧛**
- **Werewolf Pack 🐺**

Once a side is chosen, the player battles members of the opposing faction until **no enemies remain**.  
The objective is to **strategically command your chosen team to victory** by eliminating all members of the rival group.

---

## 🖥️ How It’s Played

- The game runs entirely on the **command line**
- Players interact via text-based prompts and battle results
